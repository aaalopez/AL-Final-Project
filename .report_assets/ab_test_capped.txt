### 1. What it is
A Roth IRA is a retirement savings account funded with money you have already paid taxes on. If you follow the withdrawal rules, your investment growth and withdrawals in retirement are generally tax-free.

### 2. Why it matters
A Roth IRA can help you save for retirement while potentially avoiding taxes on future withdrawals. Whether it fits your situation depends on factors such as your income, taxes, and when you may need the money. For personal guidance, consider speaking with a licensed financial advisor.
