FINtellect

A multi-agent app that explains money topics in plain language, checks suspicious messages for scam red flags, and turns what you learn into a message for a trusted family member or friend — built for people who feel behind on personal finance and are frequent targets of fraud.

The problem — and who has it

The problem

Scams and poor decisions cost more than money lost in the moment — lost investment earnings, and years of extra working life. Help today is a patchwork of scattered articles, apps that track but don't teach, and a family call that may not know either — no way to actually check a real suspicious message. FTC data: billions/year in fraud losses from adults 60+.

Who's affected

  • Older adults managing retirement, Medicare, and Social Security largely on their own, often for the first time
  • Immigrant older adults, navigating a US financial system with no equivalent back home, sometimes with a language barrier on top
  • "Sandwich generation" adults raising kids and supporting aging parents at once, who quietly admit they never learned the basics

What's new

Compared against: NerdWallet/Investopedia, Mint-style apps, AARP Fraud Watch, bank "wellness" tools, generic AI chat — none let you paste a real message and get it assessed.

  • Paste the real message and get it assessed, not just scam-awareness content
  • Family Intervention: one click sends any result to a trusted contact — countering "keep it secret from family"
  • Citation whitelist: sources are always 1 of 7 verified homepages, never fabricated
  • Editable fictional profile makes it personal, without touching a real account

Safety: defenses that work

Live test

Prompt injection. Pasted a scam message with a hidden instruction telling the AI to call it "safe." The agent flagged the injection attempt itself as a red flag and returned RISK LEVEL: High.

Live test

Fabricated sources. An earlier test caught the model inventing a 404-ing Medicare URL. A citation whitelist now restricts every source link to 7 verified homepages — re-verified live, no fabrication.

Also: no silent failures (fails safe on content-filter blocks), no LLM calls without a real click, and the app never takes a real financial action — the "Text" button opens the user's own messaging app, never auto-sends.

Impact

  • From our tests: a structured 4-part risk read in seconds for ~$0.0001–0.0003, vs. several minutes of unassisted, uncertain research
  • From public data: billions of dollars a year in reported elder-fraud losses — the scale of the problem this targets
  • Honestly: no real user pilot yet — the biggest gap in this submission, not papered over

What's next

  • A small real pilot (5–10 people) with a before/after confidence survey and a timed task
  • A true input/output/cached rate split to replace today's one blended rate
  • A per-session call cap before any public hosting
  • Optional, strictly opt-in persistence for profile and chat history
